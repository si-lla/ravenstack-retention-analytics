# Business Findings

## Overall Churn Rate

The overall churn rate was approximately 9.72%, indicating that nearly 1 in 10 customers discontinued their subscription.

## Churn by Subscription Plan

Enterprise customers showed the highest churn rate (9.98%), followed by Pro (9.67%) and Basic (9.49%).

## Churn by Industry

The highest churn rates were observed in:

* Tools (10.79%)
* Cybersecurity (10.22%)
* HealthTech (10.12%)

The lowest churn rates were observed in:

* FinTech (8.73%)
* EdTech (8.41%)

## Product Usage Analysis

Average feature usage was similar for churned and retained customers, suggesting that overall usage volume alone may not explain churn behavior.

## Error Analysis

Average product error rates showed minimal differences between churned and retained customers.

## Support Satisfaction Analysis

Customer satisfaction scores were nearly identical across churned and retained customer groups.

## Key Conclusion

The strongest churn indicators appear to be customer segment characteristics (industry and subscription plan) rather than individual usage or support metrics alone.

Future analysis should focus on customer segmentation, account health scoring, and predictive churn modeling.
